How "significant" is decided

Climate data is noisy, so a run of rising years can be luck. The Mann-Kendall test gives the probability (p-value) that a trend this consistent is pure noise. Below 0.05, scientists call it significant.

The rate comes from Sen's slope, robust to outlier years, cross-checked by linear regression.
